# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React (Vite) deployed on Vercel, domain myblok.fr. Multi-page static site: every page is prerendered to HTML at build time (`scripts/prerender.js`), head tags built in `src/seo.js`. Bilingual with language in the URL: `/fr/...` and `/en/...` with translated slugs, `/` redirects to `/fr`. Contact form posts to a Vercel Function (`api/contact.js`) that sends the email through Resend. Vercel Web Analytics (cookieless). Light and dark mode.

## Users

Primary users: decision-makers at French SMEs (PME) — owners, managers, operations leads — who are not necessarily technical and do not know tech jargon. Secondary: English-speaking / international business contacts. The site is a credibility support for outbound prospecting already underway, not an organic-traffic funnel.

## Product Purpose

Present Baptiste's independent (auto-entrepreneur) custom-software service under the brand **myblok**. He builds tailored tools and software for SMEs: automation of repetitive tasks, small business tools, and bespoke applications for SMEs that have a precise need but can't afford a large ESN (IT services firm). AI is one tool among others, used where it truly saves time — never the headline. The site must be a credible vitrine that supports direct prospecting.

## Positioning

A solo independent developer, not an agency — and this is the selling point, not something to hide: direct contact with the person who writes the code, no intermediary, no sales layer, lower cost and lighter process than a big ESN. Honesty is the differentiator.

## Operating Context

Runs the activity solo, in parallel with studies, ~5-10h/week. Currently a work-study student (alternance) at Crédit Agricole T&S as a mainframe COBOL developer — real professional-grade dev experience that lends credibility without implying scale. Based in Lyon; works on site or remotely.

Engagement process (to be shown as steps): 1) free exchange, 2) paid scoping/cadrage step (principle explained, no price shown — price discussed during the free exchange), 3) quote/devis, 4) development, 5) delivery.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Offer, expressed in three concrete blocks: automation of repetitive tasks; small business/métier tools; bespoke applications.
- Home page plus a few static pages (project case studies, legal notice, privacy policy) — no blog, no advanced features.
- Contact = simple form + email displayed in clear.
- Tone: direct, clear, PME-oriented, zero corporate bullshit / jargon.
- Bilingual FR/EN.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: **myblok** (domain myblok.fr). The name evokes "blocks" / modular blocks — may be used visually if it serves the design, without forcing it.
- Founder referred to by first name only ("Baptiste") in the body; no photo. Full legal identity belongs only in the legal notices (mentions légales).
- Design brief: very modern, strongly designed, a clear distinctive identity — explicitly NOT the generic AI look. Must avoid: cream+serif+terracotta; near-black+neon. Must ship light AND dark mode. Balance between professional/credible and startup wow-factor. Sober but dynamic, tech.

## Evidence on Hand

A few real projects already delivered (to be shown as case studies, client names only with consent). **No testimonials, no client logos, no team, no project counts, no metrics may be fabricated.** The site must stay credible without inventing social proof. Real, honest credibility only: real projects, solo independent dev, work-study COBOL developer at Crédit Agricole T&S, student in computer science.

Legal requirement (auto-entrepreneur, France): the mentions légales must display full name, statut, SIREN number, contact address, and the host — to be completed by Baptiste (SIREN is a placeholder to fill).

## Product Principles

1. Honesty is the strategy — solo and small, framed as an advantage (direct contact, no intermediary), never hidden.
2. Speak to the SME, not to developers — concrete situations and outcomes, no jargon.
3. Prove through clarity, not fake proof — no invented testimonials, team, or numbers.
4. Low friction to contact — the path from "this is for me" to "get in touch" stays short throughout the scroll.
5. Distinctive by design — a memorable, non-generic identity that reflects the "blok" name.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Bilingual FR/EN. Keep text legible and contrast sufficient in both light and dark modes; the site must remain usable and clear for non-technical business readers.
